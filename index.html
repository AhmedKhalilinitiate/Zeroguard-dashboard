How to interpret results

Version match: a product identity and comparable affected-version rule matched; check platform, feature and configuration applicability before remediation. Needs verification: the product was identified but the available evidence or version comparison is insufficient to confirm impact.

Unlisted products, unknown versions, complex CPE AND/OR logic and ecosystem-specific version semantics may require manual validation. This tool does not scan hosts, discover installed versions, transmit your inventory or assert that a host is exploitable.

Using ZeroGuard

Evidence-aware cybersecurity triage for IT, OT, and security operations.

Interpreting threat information

KEV means CISA has evidence of exploitation. It does not alone prove pre-patch (“zero-day”) exploitation. CVSS estimates intrinsic vulnerability severity, not your business impact. EPSS estimates the probability of exploitation in the next 30 days using FIRST's statistical model. Scores can be unavailable.

Risk-based triage: start with KEV membership, then assess affected asset inventory, exposure, business criticality and vendor remediation requirements. The priority sort in this app is a convenience heuristic, not an official risk score.

OT/ICS: category matching is indicative only. Check CISA ICS advisories, vendor bulletins, affected firmware, OT change control and maintenance windows.

Indicators: URLs may be dangerous. The interface deliberately does not make malicious links clickable.

Updates: GitHub Actions builds a source-attributed data snapshot every six hours. The browser checks for an updated snapshot every 15 minutes when open. Scheduler delays and failed upstream feeds can occur; see Source health.

Privacy: Personal bookmarks remain in local browser storage; there is no account-based storage or telemetry in the app.
